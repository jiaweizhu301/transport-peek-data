Stops on a street that forms a suburb boundary may be assigned to either side.

Modifications

CC BY 4.0 requires that changes be indicated. The published timetable databases are a transformation of the upstream GTFS feeds, not a verbatim copy:

  • Converted from GTFS text files to SQLite, and gzip-compressed.
  • Occupancy and vehicle files dropped. Route shapes are published as separate per-mode files, simplified (Douglas-Peucker, 5 m) and delta-encoded.
  • Sydney Trains: trips belonging to the NSWTrains agency removed (they duplicate a separate feed).
  • Buses: school-bus routes (route_type 712) and the one ferry route carried in the bus feed (route_type 4) removed.
  • Light rail: the three light rail feeds (L1; L2 and L3; L4) are combined into one database. Their identifiers do not overlap and are kept as they are.
  • Trips that serve no boarding stop (empty running) removed; stop times where the vehicle neither picks up nor sets down removed.
  • Trips that cannot actually be ridden removed: a trip is kept only if, after the first stop where you can board, there is a later stop where you can get off.
  • The last stop of every trip is marked as not boardable (pickup_type set to 1), so a terminating service is not listed as a departure there. Drop-off at the last stop is unchanged.
  • Trips labelled “Empty Train” (or a similar non-revenue label) that do let passengers board are kept, and their headsign is replaced by the name of the last stop where passengers can get off.
  • Platform numbers parsed out of platform stop names into a platform_code column.
  • Repeated stop sequences de-duplicated into a stop_patterns table.
  • The service calendar is trimmed to a rolling window that starts the day before the build date (or on the first day the feed covers, if that is later).

Times, stop identifiers, route identifiers and trip identifiers are preserved unchanged, so the data remains cross-referenceable with the upstream feeds.

做了哪些改动

CC BY 4.0 要求标明改动。发布的时刻表数据库是对上游 GTFS 的加工，不是原样搬运：

  • GTFS 文本文件转成 SQLite，再 gzip 压缩。
  • 丢弃 occupancies、vehicle_* 等文件；线路走向（shapes）按 mode 单独发布，经 Douglas-Peucker 5 米抽稀、差分编码。
  • 火车：裁掉 NSWTrains 机构的车次（与另一个独立 feed 重复）。
  • 公交：裁掉校车线路（route_type 712），以及混在公交 feed 里的那一条渡轮线路（route_type 4）。
  • 轻轨：三个轻轨 feed（L1；L2 与 L3；L4）合并成一个库。它们的 ID 互不重叠，原样保留。
  • 裁掉全程不上客的班次（空车调度），以及既不上客也不下客的停站行（飞站）。
  • 裁掉实际坐不了的班次：在第一个可上客的站之后，还得有一个可下客的站，这趟车才保留。
  • 每个班次的末站标成不可上客（pickup_type 改为 1），终到的车不会在终点站被列成出发；末站的下客不变。
  • 标着「Empty Train」（或类似非营运字样）、但实际可以上客的班次保留，车头方向改写成最后一个可下客的站名。
  • 从站台子站名里解析出站台号，写进 platform_code 列。
  • 重复的停站序列归一化到 stop_patterns 表。
  • 服务日历裁成从建库前一天起的滚动窗口（若 feed 本身从更晚的日子开始，则从 feed 的第一天起）。

时刻、站点 ID、线路 ID、车次 ID 全部保持原值，因此仍可与上游数据互相对照。
