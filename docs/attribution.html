Suburb boundaries

© Australian Bureau of Statistics, CC BY 4.0. The “find stops by suburb” list is derived from the ABS Australian Statistical Geography Standard (ASGS) Edition 3, Suburbs and Localities (SAL) 2021, GDA2020 digital boundary files, licensed under CC BY 4.0. Changes: NSW suburbs intersecting the bus network only, empty geometries removed, “(NSW)” suffixes shortened, boundaries simplified (Douglas-Peucker, 5 m), and each bus stop assigned to the suburb containing it. Stops on a street that forms a suburb boundary may be assigned to either side.

Modifications

CC BY 4.0 requires that changes be indicated. The published timetable databases are a transformation of the upstream GTFS feeds, not a verbatim copy:

  • Converted from GTFS text files to SQLite, and gzip-compressed.
  • Occupancy and vehicle files dropped. Route shapes are published as separate per-mode files, simplified (Douglas-Peucker, 5 m) and delta-encoded.
  • Sydney Trains: trips belonging to the NSWTrains agency removed (they duplicate a separate feed).
  • Trips that serve no boarding stop (empty running) removed; stop times where the vehicle neither picks up nor sets down removed.
  • Platform numbers parsed out of platform stop names into a platform_code column.
  • Repeated stop sequences de-duplicated into a stop_patterns table.
  • The service calendar is trimmed to a rolling window starting at the build date.

Times, stop identifiers, route identifiers and trip identifiers are preserved unchanged, so the data remains cross-referenceable with the upstream feeds.

区界数据

© Australian Bureau of Statistics，CC BY 4.0。「按区找站」来自 ABS 澳大利亚统计地理标准（ASGS） 第 3 版 Suburbs and Localities（SAL）2021 GDA2020 边界文件，按 CC BY 4.0 授权。改动：只取与公交网络相交的 NSW 区， 去掉空几何，缩短「(NSW)」后缀，边界抽稀（Douglas-Peucker，5 米），并把每个公交站归到包含它的区。 站在作为区界的街上时，可能被归到街的任一侧。

做了哪些改动

CC BY 4.0 要求标明改动。发布的时刻表数据库是对上游 GTFS 的加工，不是原样搬运：

  • GTFS 文本文件转成 SQLite，再 gzip 压缩。
  • 丢弃 occupancies、vehicle_* 等文件；线路走向（shapes）按 mode 单独发布，经 Douglas-Peucker 5 米抽稀、差分编码。
  • 火车：裁掉 NSWTrains 机构的车次（与另一个独立 feed 重复）。
  • 裁掉全程不上客的班次（空车调度），以及既不上客也不下客的停站行（飞站）。
  • 从站台子站名里解析出站台号，写进 platform_code 列。
  • 重复的停站序列归一化到 stop_patterns 表。
  • 服务日历裁成从建库当天起的滚动窗口。

时刻、站点 ID、线路 ID、车次 ID 全部保持原值，因此仍可与上游数据互相对照。
